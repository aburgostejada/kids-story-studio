# Prompt: direct one act

Used once per act file (three agents in parallel), after the modules are assembled into the film.

---

You are working on "<title>", a fully procedural kids short. The modules are built; the film is assembled by
`film/shots.js` (story state, `scene()`, helpers on `P.SH`) and one file per act (`B[name] = (u, T) => scene`).
Read `film/CONTRACT.md` (module contract and the staging bible: story, pace, shot table with narration timings,
places, screen direction, framing rules), `film/shots.js`, then your own file.

Your job: direct and stage these shots so each is the best version of its beat: <shot list with film times>.
For every shot decide the camera (position, lens, focus, aperture, movement), the blocking (who is where, on which side,
at what depth), the acting (poses, eyes, brows, mouths, blinks, squash, secondary motion, timing) and any effects, plus
per-shot key and rim lights so characters read in the dark. Make sure each narration line is illustrated while it
plays. Check every shot at its start, middle, end and key actions, and fix what you see: faces blocked, characters too
small or cut by the frame edge, characters sinking into the ground, props in the wrong place, flat composition, dead
holds, feet skating. Keep continuity with the neighbouring shots.

You own only <act file>. If you need a change in a file you do not own, describe it under "requests" in your report.

Final report: per shot, what you changed and the film times you checked; requests for other files; remaining
weaknesses, honestly.
