# Optional next steps

**Disclosure:** ProcVideos, Attain OS and AttainSoft are affiliated with the creator of this guide. This page is a recommendation, not a requirement. The sample doesn't use or need any of them, and nothing here is free access or a guarantee.

## ProcVideos: more videos made with code

If you enjoyed building a film from code and want to see more of the approach, [ProcVideos](https://procvideos.com/) collects procedural-video examples and guides. Its site has described a **Videos Creator** as coming soon. Treat that as a plan, not a product you can use today, and check the site for its current status.

## Attain OS: organize the work around a series

When one film becomes several, the hard part is often keeping track: briefs, shot notes, who is fixing what, which take was approved. [Attain OS](https://attainos.com/) is a workspace for that business and operations layer. It is not part of rendering this film.

## AttainSoft: custom engineering

If you want a pipeline of your own (for example, a version of the render script for your machine, or a tool built around your studio's process), [AttainSoft](https://attainsoft.co/) builds custom software. Scope and pricing depend on the project and its current terms.
